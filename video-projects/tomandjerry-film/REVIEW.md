# Review — TomandJerry.ai internship promo (16 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps, silent loop)

- Creative calls: built from the real tomandjerry.ai capture (headline, Tom/Jerry panes and their real opening
  questions, feature copy, brand colours #B45F09 / #0C7A6D). Pastel worlds: lavender hook/end, Limencello for Tom,
  Summer Green for Jerry. EB Garamond stands in for the site's Hoefler Text.
- Outputs (CV, mock interview, job board, brief, shortlist, screening) are simple illustrated cards carrying the
  site's own feature wording, not screenshots of product screens (none were public).
- Measured: `check` (no errors; collage cards only overlap while flying in). Looked at: beat sheet and 4 proof frames.
- Kept on purpose: no sound (it plays muted on a TV in the portfolio). Candidate names are generic (A/B/C).
- Weakest shot: Jerry's scene before the outputs land (empty pane for ~1 s).
