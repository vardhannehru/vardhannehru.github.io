[h2] About the School
[p] One school for the whole of a child’s education, so there is no
      hunting for a new place at Class VI or Class XI, and no starting over with
      new friends.
[h3] One School, Every Year
[p] Nursery through Class XII on a single campus. Children keep their teachers,
           their friends and their routine.
[h3] CBSE Affiliated
[p] A national curriculum that travels with the family and lines up with the
           entrance examinations that follow Class XII.
