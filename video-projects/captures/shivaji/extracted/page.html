A CBSE education, from Nursery to Class XII

Shivaji Vidya Peeth High School is a co-educational, English medium school in Kavuri Hills, Madhapur. A child can join us in Nursery and leave after the Class XII board examination without ever changing school.

Welcome to Our School

Our motto is Pride of Excellence, and we mean it plainly: steady teaching, proper practical work, and room for children to grow into more than a set of marks. We follow the curriculum of the Central Board of Secondary Education under affiliation number 3630013.

About the School

One school for the whole of a child’s education, so there is no hunting for a new place at Class VI or Class XI, and no starting over with new friends.

One School, Every Year

Nursery through Class XII on a single campus. Children keep their teachers, their friends and their routine.

CBSE Affiliated

A national curriculum that travels with the family and lines up with the entrance examinations that follow Class XII.

More Than Marks

Sport, arts and activity sit alongside academics rather than after them.
